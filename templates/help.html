Help

Quick answers for inspectors, teachers, parents and district officers.

For inspectors

  1. Open Dashboard → click Verify next to a school.
  2. Scan fingerprint on the reader (or Simulate in demo).
  3. Click Capture GPS — you must be within 200 m of the school.
  4. Click Start camera, then Take snapshot.
  5. Enter the number of students actually present, then Submit.

If a step fails, the banner at the bottom tells you exactly what's missing.

Reading a status pill

Verified all checks passed at the last inspection

Flagged at least one check failed — see Inspection history

Unverified never inspected, or inspection data is stale

The audit ledger

Every action is hashed and chained. If someone edits the database directly, the chain breaks and the audit page shows the exact event number.

District officers and inspectors can view it. Parents cannot.

For parents

Your report is anonymous. We never store your IP address in plain form.

Reports are used to triangulate — one report alone doesn't trigger action, but a pattern does.
